## Common issues

**PlanIt asks me to sign in to iCloud**  
PlanIt uses Apple **CloudKit** to sync events and profiles. Sign in to iCloud under Settings → \[your name\] and ensure iCloud is enabled for this device.

**I don’t see my events after reinstalling or switching devices**  
Events are tied to your **PlanIt username** and **iCloud account**. Make sure you’re signed into the same Apple ID you used before.

**Invites or usernames don’t appear as expected**  
Invitees must use **PlanIt usernames** (letters, numbers, underscores). Handles are matched against profiles stored with CloudKit.

**Something still broken**  
Email us using the address above with as much detail as you can—we’ll investigate.
