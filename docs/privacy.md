# Privacy Policy for PlanIt

**Last updated:** 2026-05-05  
**Developer:** Elise Wong-McBride  
**Support contact:** elise.wongmcbride@gmail.com

PlanIt is an event planning app that lets you create events, invite people by PlanIt username, and respond with times that work.

## Summary
- PlanIt uses **Apple iCloud / CloudKit** to store and sync your data.
- PlanIt does **not** sell your data.
- PlanIt does **not** use third‑party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Data PlanIt Collects
PlanIt collects and stores the following information to provide the app’s functionality:

- **Account information**
  - Your **PlanIt username** (chosen by you)
  - A device/account identifier provided by Apple’s iCloud services (used internally to associate your PlanIt profile with your iCloud account)

- **Event content you create or provide**
  - Event name and description
  - Invitees (PlanIt usernames)
  - Proposed time blocks and responses

## How Your Data Is Used
PlanIt uses the data above to:
- Create and display events you create or are invited to
- Let invitees submit availability responses
- Sync your events and responses across your devices using iCloud / CloudKit

## Where Your Data Is Stored
PlanIt stores app data in **Apple CloudKit**. Data is transmitted to and stored by Apple as part of iCloud services. PlanIt does not operate its own separate server for this data.

## Sharing
PlanIt shares event information with people you invite:
- When you add an invitee username to an event, that event becomes visible to that invitee inside PlanIt.
- Invitees can see event details and proposed times in order to respond.

PlanIt does not otherwise share or sell personal data to third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion
PlanIt retains your data in CloudKit until you delete it (where the app provides deletion controls) or until it is removed as part of normal iCloud/CloudKit operation.

If you would like help deleting your PlanIt username/profile or event data, contact **elise.wongmcbride@gmail.com**.

## Your Choices
- You can choose what event information to create and share with invitees.
- You can stop using PlanIt at any time.
- You can manage iCloud settings in iOS Settings (e.g., sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud access for the app), which may affect the app’s ability to sync data.

## Children’s Privacy
PlanIt is not designed for use by children under 13. PlanIt does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date will reflect the latest version.
